About the Project

Who we are

AutoEncyclopedia is a large, independent automotive knowledge catalog. The project was born out of a passion for vehicles and the need to give every buyer reliable, unbiased data.

Our data

We use: the Wikipedia REST API (descriptions), Wikimedia Commons (CC-BY-SA images), the NHTSA VIN Decoder API (technical data, US government), and the NHTSA Complaints API (owner complaints and known faults).

Privacy Policy

Data controller

The controller of personal data is Olivier Rafalo (AutoEncyclopedia Engine) — contact: rafaloolivier1@gmail.com. Data is processed in accordance with the GDPR (EU 2016/679) and, where it concerns users based in Switzerland, the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

What data we collect

Depending on how you use the service, we may process the following data:

  • Google OAuth 2.0 login data: name, email address, profile picture.
  • Phone number: only if voluntarily provided when using contact features.
  • Location data: an approximate location (e.g. city/region) used only to show regionally relevant content.
  • Payment data (Stripe): for a PRO subscription, payments are handled by Stripe, Inc. We do not store card numbers — they are processed directly by Stripe under its own privacy policy and the PCI-DSS standard.
  • VIN numbers: not stored — sent directly and anonymously to the public NHTSA.gov (US government) API.
  • Cookies: see the section below.

Cookies

The service uses essential Supabase authentication session cookies (required for login to work) and, once you consent, third-party cookies including Stripe (for payments). Your choice is remembered locally in your browser.

Legal basis and purpose of processing

We process data based on: user consent (login, cookies), necessity for performance of a contract (PRO subscription), and the controller's legitimate interest (security, fraud prevention).

Data sharing

Data may be shared with third parties only to the extent necessary to provide the service: Supabase (hosting, authentication), Stripe (payments), Google (OAuth login). Data is not sold or shared for marketing purposes without separate consent.

Your rights

You may also file a complaint with your national data protection authority (e.g. Poland's PUODO) or, for Swiss users, the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

Retention period
