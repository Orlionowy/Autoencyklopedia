About the Project

Who we are

AutoEncyclopedia is a free, independent catalog of automotive knowledge. The project was built out of a passion for vehicles and a desire to give every buyer reliable, easy-to-understand data before they hand over their money.

Our data sources

We draw on: the Wikipedia REST API (descriptions), Wikimedia Commons (CC-BY-SA photos), the NHTSA VIN Decoder API (technical data, US government), the NHTSA Complaints API (owner complaints and faults), and the CarVector API (verified specs, representative images, and federal recall data).

A note on accuracy

Reliability scores, "avoid this" notes, and typical-cost ranges on this site are editorial estimates meant as a starting point for research, not verified guarantees. Recall and complaint data pulled live from NHTSA and CarVector is sourced and dated; always confirm details against your vehicle's own service history before buying.
